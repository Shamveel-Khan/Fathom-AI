## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an engineered software aesthetic built for high-density, analytical workflows. It embodies precision, architectural discipline, and calm authority. Designed for technical founders, product teams, and executive operators reviewing AI-distilled conversation data, the interface reduces cognitive noise while maximizing scanning velocity.

The style fuses **Engineered Software Minimalism** with **Subtle Luminescence**. Rather than employing simulated physical drop shadows or decorative consumer gradients, depth is established through a calibrated graphite luminance ramp, structural hairline borders, and translucent surface overlays. The interface prioritizes deep contrast ratios, monospace telemetry metadata, and dense analytical widgets alongside expansive macro-rhythms.

## Colors

The system is calibrated strictly for dark-mode environments. Surfaces reflect architectural discipline, ascending through progressive graphite luminance tiers from `#010102` up to `#28282c`.

- **Primary Action (`#ffffff`)**: The primary CTA breaks dark-mode field continuity as an inverted, stark white slab with `#08090a` label text, guaranteeing instant focal recognition without requiring loud chromatic accents.
- **Brand & Interaction (`#5e6ad2`, `#828fff`, `#7170ff`)**: A restrained indigo spectrum. `#5e6ad2` anchors brand-level assets, filled brand actions, and key interactive highlights, while `#828fff` and `#7170ff` deliver high-contrast text links, glowing vector paths, and status telemetry.
- **Ink Ladder**: Content readability uses a 4-step hierarchy: `#f7f8f8` for primary headings and active inputs, `#d0d6e0` for conversational transcript body text, `#8a8f98` for timestamps and metadata eyebrows, and `#62666d` for structural captions and inactive state indicators.
- **Domain Pillars**: Semantic pillars partition analytical insights into distinct cognitive domains: `#68cc58` (Planning), `#d4b144` (Execution & Building), and `#7a7fad` (Governance & Security).

## Typography

Typography relies on precise weights and disciplined negative tracking to ensure clarity at high data densities. 

The primary family is configured with variable axis weights at **510** (Medium-Plus) and **590** (Semibold-Plus) to deliver typographic structure without optical bloat. All display scales feature negative letter spacing (`-0.022em`) to bind headline rhythm tightly.

The technical companion stack provides strict tabular and character alignment across playback scrubbers, video timecodes, confidence metrics, and keyboard shortcuts. Body text remains locked at `15px` across viewports to preserve reading stamina during extended transcript review sessions.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout utilizes an intentional dynamic between high-density component internals and expansive macro-spatial bands. The system operates on a base 4px metric, with primary interactive dimensions scaling on 8px intervals.

- **Grid Architecture**: 12-column responsive fluid grid pinned to a maximum content container of `1200px` (or `1024px` for dedicated reading panels). The hero narrative measure is capped at `640px` to maintain optimal line lengths.
- **Component Intervals**: Inner form controls, waveform controls, and telemetry pills employ `space-xs` (8px) and `space-sm` (12px). Card containers and transcript insight modules scale to `space-lg` (24px) internal padding.
- **Section Rhythm**: Page layouts use macro bands of `96px` (`spacing.section`) to `160px` (`spacing.section-lg`) on desktop viewports, creating clear visual separation without reliance on heavy decorative separators.
- **Responsive Behavior**: Mobile viewports (<640px) collapse multi-column split views (e.g., video player alongside transcript feed) into single-column vertical stacks. Control targets enforce a physical touch minimum of `44px` while preserving visual element bounds.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses an **Inverted Graphite Elevation Scale** coupled with **Translucent Luminescence**. Elements do not cast heavy downward drop shadows; instead, elevated surfaces rise closer to an overhead white light source by taking on lighter hex values and crisp borders.

- **Level 0 (Floor Canvas)**: `#010102` deep canvas on marketing bands and `#08090a` on operational workspaces.
- **Level 1 (Panels & Frames)**: `#0f1011` panels surrounded by a `1px solid #23252a` structural hairline. Used for video containers, audio scrubbers, and docked sidebars.
- **Level 2 (Translucent Containers)**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` fill bounded by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Used for secondary actions and glass cards.
- **Level 3 (Raised Controls & Keycaps)**: `#1c1c1f` rising to `#232326` and `#28282c` on active focus. Accented with an edge-defining `1px solid #3e3e44` top highlight on interactive keys.
- **Level 4 (Floating Overlays)**: `rgba(11, 11, 11, 0.8)` layered with a `12px` to `16px` backdrop filter blur for fixed navigation headers, floating transport bars, and contextual command menus.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture balances modern utility with compact engineering. Roundedness values are calibrated to reinforce control affordances:

- **Core Controls (`8px` / `rounded.md`)**: Buttons, text input boxes, dropdown selectors, and media playback control clusters utilize a consistent 8px radius.
- **Structural Modules (`12px` - `16px` / `rounded.lg` - `rounded.xl`)**: Transcript insight panels, video player surfaces, AI meeting summary modules, and code inspection blocks use a 12px or 16px radius.
- **Micro Affordances (`4px` - `6px` / `rounded.xs` - `rounded.sm`)**: Keyboard shortcut keycaps (`keyboard-key`) and timecode markers use a disciplined 4px radius with crisp structural hairlines.
- **Pills (`9999px`)**: Reserved exclusively for dynamic status markers, sentiment pills, and live recording badges. Primary and secondary interactive buttons never use pill geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Inverted stark solid white (`#ffffff`) background with canvas dark text (`#08090a`), `36px` height, `8px` corner radius, `0 16px` padding, typography at `15px / weight 510`. Hover state gently dims opacity to `0.92`.
- **Secondary Glass Action**: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` fill bounded by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text in `#f7f8f8`. Hover shifts fill to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Brand Action**: Filled `#5e6ad2` with pure `#ffffff` text, used for high-intent AI operations.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Single-line controls fixed at `36px` height, background `#1c1c1f`, surrounded by `1px solid #34343a`. Text set to `#f7f8f8` with placeholder text in `#62666d`.
- Active focus transitions the border to `#5e6ad2` with an ambient `0 0 0 1px #5e6ad2` ring.

### Chips, Badges & Keyboard Keys
- **Keyboard Shortcut (`keyboard-key`)**: Sits on `#232326`, border `1px solid #34343a`, top-edge highlight `1px solid #3e3e44`, radius `4px`, padding `2px 6px`. Rendered in monospace `11px` with `#d0d6e0`.
- **Status / Pillar Badges**: Rendered as pill shapes with `accent-tint` (`#18182f`) background and `#828fff` text for system updates, or pillar-specific backgrounds (e.g., `#68cc58` at `10%` alpha for plan indicators).

### Cards & Container Panels
- Base structural card rendered with `#0f1011` fill, enclosed by `1px solid #23252a` hairline border, `12px` corner radius, and `24px` internal padding.

### Meeting Waveform & Audio Player
- Embedded scrub track on `#141516` surface, track base in `#23252a`, active playback progress illuminated in `#7170ff`. Speaker markers indicated via domain pillar colored pins (`#68cc58`, `#d4b144`, `#7a7fad`).

### Interactive Transcript Reader
- Multi-row layout on `#08090a` canvas. Active spoken segment highlighted with a `2px` left border in `#828fff` and subtle background wash `rgba(130, 143, 255, 0.04)`. Timestamps rendered in technical monospace `#8a8f98`.
